# Conference Format Compliance — IEEE Template

## Source of Truth

When an IEEE conference LaTeX template is supplied by the user, follow that exact template and do not invent venue-specific formatting rules.

For the supplied template, the paper uses `IEEEtran` conference mode. The template explicitly says not to alter prescribed margins, column widths, line spacing, or fonts, and asks authors to observe page limits. It also warns that all template guidance text must be removed before submission.

## Title and Abstract

- Do not use symbols, special characters, footnotes, or math in the paper title or abstract when the supplied template explicitly prohibits them.
- Keep the title informative and specific.
- Do not leave template title/author/funding placeholders in the final paper.

## Authors and Affiliations

- Preserve the author order supplied by the authors.
- Keep affiliations concise and consistent.
- Ensure author count and affiliation information match the actual submission.

## Acronyms

- Define abbreviations and acronyms the first time they are used in the text.
- Avoid abbreviations in the title or section headings unless unavoidable.

## Units and Equations

- Prefer one coherent unit system, with SI encouraged by the template.
- Use a leading zero for decimals.
- Define symbols in or immediately around the relevant equation.
- Number equations consecutively when numbering is needed.
- Use soft cross-references rather than hard-coded equation numbers.

## Figures and Tables

- Place figures/tables at the top or bottom of columns where possible.
- Large figures/tables may span both columns when appropriate.
- Put figure captions below figures.
- Put table heads/captions above tables.
- Cite figures and tables before they appear.
- Use `Fig.~\ref{...}` for figure references.
- Figure axis labels should use clear words, with units in parentheses where applicable.

## References

- Number citations consecutively.
- Follow the citation punctuation style in the template.
- Keep bibliography entries accurate and complete.
- Do not use placeholder references in the submitted manuscript.

## Final Template Cleanup

Search the manuscript for obvious template residue such as:

- `Conference Paper Title`
- `Given Name Surname`
- `dept. name of organization`
- `City, Country`
- `email address`
- template sample paragraphs
- sample tables/figures
- funding placeholders
- the template's red warning text

The supplied template specifically states that template text must be removed before submission.
