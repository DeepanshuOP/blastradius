# Abstract Writing Guide

## Goal

Write a strong abstract by first deciding the logic, then revising it repeatedly.

## Pre-Writing Questions

1. What technical problem do we solve, and why is there no well-established solution?
2. What is our technical contribution?
3. Why can the method work in essence?
4. What technical advantage or new insight does it provide?

## Preferred Structure

1. Task.
2. Technical challenge for previous methods.
3. Insight or technical contribution.
4. Benefit / technical advantage.
5. Experimental evidence.

When there are multiple contributions, present each contribution together with its technical advantage instead of listing disconnected features.

## Rules

- Discuss previous work only around the challenge the paper actually solves.
- Keep technical names understandable without forcing the reader to guess their meaning.
- Do not introduce claims that are not supported by the experiments.
- Keep the abstract concise and specific.
