# Does My Writing Flow?

Use the source principle from the original skill's flow guide:

1. Pretend you are the reader.
2. Ask whether the vocabulary, paragraph logic, and thesis connection are understandable to someone who did not write the paper.
3. Reverse-outline the paper: thesis -> topic sentences -> evidence/explanation.
4. Revise paragraphs that do not map cleanly to the thesis.
5. Use transitions to make relationships between ideas visible.

## Practical Research-Paper Questions

- Does this paragraph have one explicit message?
- Does the first sentence tell me what the paragraph is doing?
- Can I understand the technical nouns without hidden context?
- Does each sentence connect to the previous sentence?
- Does the paragraph support the section's purpose?
- Can I summarize the section in a short reverse outline?
