# Paper Review

## Goal

Use an adversarial reviewer mindset to detect rejection risks before submission.

## Critical Rule

Every major claim, especially in the Abstract and Introduction, must be technically correct and supported by appropriate evidence or citation.

## Five Core Rejection Dimensions

### 1. Contribution

- What new knowledge does the paper provide?
- Is the problem meaningful?
- Is the technical idea more than routine practice?
- Is the main finding informative or merely predictable?

### 2. Writing Clarity

- Can a knowledgeable reader understand and reproduce the method?
- Is the motivation of every key module clear?
- Are terms and notation consistent?
- Does each paragraph have one message and clear flow?

### 3. Experimental Strength

- Are comparisons against strong baselines meaningful?
- Is absolute performance competitive enough for the target setting?
- Are gains consistent across relevant settings?
- Are failures reported honestly?

### 4. Evaluation Completeness

- Are important ablations present?
- Are strong/recent baselines covered?
- Are metrics and protocols sufficient?
- Are scenarios challenging enough to support the conclusions?

### 5. Method Design Soundness

- Are assumptions realistic?
- Are there hidden technical defects?
- Is the method robust without excessive special-case tuning?
- Do benefits outweigh complexity and new limitations?

## Added Integrity Dimensions

### 6. Human Authorship / Originality

- Does the wording reflect the author's understanding and voice?
- Is any paragraph suspiciously close to source material?
- Has the paper avoided detector-gaming behavior?

### 7. Provenance / Citation Integrity

- Can important claims be traced to an experiment or source?
- Are cited papers real and relevant?
- Are literature values clearly separated from reproduced results?

### 8. Conference Compliance

- Does the manuscript follow the supplied template?
- Is all template guidance text removed?
- Are figures, tables, references, author information, title, and abstract compliant?

## Adversarial Workflow

1. Read as a skeptical reviewer.
2. Ask what could cause rejection.
3. Tie every concern to evidence.
4. Mark each item `pass`, `needs revision`, or `needs new evidence`.
5. Revise.
6. Repeat until remaining weaknesses are explicit and acceptable.
