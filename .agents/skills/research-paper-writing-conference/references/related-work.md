# Related Work Writing Guide

## Goal

Position the work against the most relevant research and make the technical distinction easy to verify.

## Workflow

1. List the most directly competing and recent papers.
2. Group literature by technical topic, not only by year.
3. For each topic, summarize the common paradigm.
4. State the limitation that matters for the paper's target challenge.
5. End by clarifying the technical distinction of the present work.

## Paragraph Template

1. Topic sentence.
2. Representative methods.
3. Relevant limitation or unresolved challenge.
4. Transition to the gap addressed by the paper.

## Rules

- Do not make Related Work a citation dump.
- Do not hide the strongest competitors.
- Do not claim superiority merely because a paper uses a different technique.
- Keep every citation attached to a claim it actually supports.
