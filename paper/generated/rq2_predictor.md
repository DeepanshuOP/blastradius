## Gate 3 verdict

Gate 3 (ROADMAP §37.1) is NOT MET: on the 148 test instances the ranker's Recall@10 is 0.525 (95% bootstrap CI [0.447, 0.599]) against 0.522 for the best baseline (B7 readily-available-info LTR); Holm-adjusted Wilcoxon p = 0.3823, Cliff's delta = +0.030 (negligible). The criterion is: higher mean, adjusted p < 0.05 and |delta| >= 0.147.
