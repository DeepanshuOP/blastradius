# E1 rankers under the metrics of Predictive Test Selection (Machalica et al., ICSE-SEIP 2019)

Generated by `analysis/pts_comparison.py` at git `a29310d2262b513c4612f20e98179c70936f7d89`.

Test split D-57 (chronological, 148 changes). Unit: test FILE; candidates: every test file of the repo at that commit, as in E1. Selecting at rate r takes the top ceil(r x candidates) files of each change (exact arithmetic). TestRecall is micro (sum of hits over sum of failing files) unless marked macro (mean of per-change ratios). Seeded methods (ours, B7, B6: seeds 0-4; B1: 20 seeds) report the mean over seeds. This measures rankers trained and evaluated on BR-Bench, a different corpus and unit than the base paper's industrial setting; the numbers are not comparable to theirs in absolute terms.
